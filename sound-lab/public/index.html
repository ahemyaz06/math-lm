Quick Test Presets

What Each Sound Means (Click to Preview)

  • Continuous Dark Bass Outline: Stays on in the background and holds your current point when you stop. Rises as you move forward in X and lowers as you move backward so you always feel direction and connection.
  • Warm Low Step Pulse: Plays every 0.5 or 1.0 units when the curve is below zero (negative Y).
  • Clear Treble Step Pulse: Plays every 0.5 or 1.0 units when the curve is above zero (positive Y).
  • Center Marimba / Wood Tick: Crossing Y = 0 or the Origin (0, 0).
  • Harmonic Swell: High fifth bloom at a Peak (local max); warm low fifth at a Valley (local min).
  • Firm Wood Tap: Closed / marked solid point on the curve (toggle with P).
  • Soft Hollow Air Gap: Background bass dips out and a soft breathy gap plays because the point is missing (toggle with H).
